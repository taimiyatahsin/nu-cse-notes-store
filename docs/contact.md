## What you can contact me about

- **Website improvements:** Suggest a feature, design change, or anything that would make the site easier to use.
- **Bug reports:** Let me know if a page, link, calculator, search, or other feature is not working correctly.
- **Study PDFs and materials:** Request a subject PDF, notes, question resources, or other useful CSE study materials.
- **Corrections and feedback:** Share inaccurate information, missing topics, or ideas for making the resources better.
