# NU CSE First Semester Syllabus Reference

This page records the First Semester course structure from the National University CSE (Honours) syllabus document titled **“Computer Science and Engineering, CSE (Hon's) Syllabus 2017–2018.”**

National University lists this document in its official **Syllabus (Professionals)** archive, dated 31 October 2018. This is a historical syllabus reference. Its listing does not independently establish that it is the applicable syllabus for the 2025–26 admission session or any later session.

> **Session warning:** Do not treat the course list below as confirmed for your own session until your department verifies it against the syllabus/approved course outline supplied for your admission batch.
