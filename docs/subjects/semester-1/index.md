## How to use this section

1. Select any subject card to open that subject's dedicated overview.
2. Use its chapter links to study individual topics.
3. Check revision guides and question resources before exams.
4. Use the site search to find a specific topic across the study portal.

**Navigation note:** Opening the First Semester section now leads to this subject directory. SPL will open only when you select the SPL card or its subject link.
